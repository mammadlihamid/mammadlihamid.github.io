Hamid Mammadli
Electronics Engineering Student

Hamid,

I' m an 18-year-old Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Istanbul Technical University (ITU). I currently serve as a member of the electronics subteam for the ITU AUV (Autonomous Underwater Vehicle) team, where I apply my skills in hardware design and system integration. My expertise includes PCB design using KiCad, working with embedded systems like NVIDIA Jetson and STM32, and developing autonomous solutions for marine robotics.My background is rooted in competitive engineering, highlighted by winning First Place and the "Most Original Design" award at TEKNOFEST 2024 for Unmanned Underwater Systems. I have also secured top honors at the STEAM Azerbaijan Festival 2023 and the Future's Scientists XIV robotics category. Beyond robotics, I am proficient in Python, C++, and C#, and I have a strong interest in Cyber Security, having led teams in CTF competitions.
